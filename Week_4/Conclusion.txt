5. Final Project Conclusion & Retrospective

    Across the four-week curriculum, this project progressed from raw tabular data to an operational, production-viable artificial intelligence solution:
        
        1. Week 1: Built a solid foundation through data cleaning, handling hidden missing values, categorical encoding, and feature scaling.
        2. Week 2: Benchmarked four supervised algorithms, identifying Random Forest as the superior architectural family.
        3. Week 3: Discovered three distinct behavioral customer personas via K-Means and Hierarchical Clustering, and tuned the Random Forest model to exceed 75% Recall and 0.85 ROC-AUC.
        4. Week 4: Serialized the full pipeline and deployed an interactive, type-safe FastAPI prediction microservice.

    This end-to-end system delivers quantifiable business value by transforming reactive customer support into a proactive, automated retention engine.
