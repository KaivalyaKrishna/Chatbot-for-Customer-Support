4. Performance Metrics & Comparative Evaluation

    All models were evaluated strictly on the unseen testing set (1,409 samples). Below is the comprehensive metric comparison:

    Random Forest Classifier:

        Accuracy: 80.20%
        Precision: 66.30%
        Recall: 54.81%
        F1-Score: 0.6001
        ROC-AUC: 0.8445
        Assessment: Top-performing champion model across all critical discriminative metrics.

    Logistic Regression:

        Accuracy: 80.06%
        Precision: 65.48%
        Recall: 54.28%
        F1-Score: 0.5934
        ROC-AUC: 0.8418
        Assessment: Strong, competitive linear baseline with performance closely matching the ensemble forest.

    Decision Tree (Max Depth = 5):

        Accuracy: 78.78%
        Precision: 62.42%
        Recall: 51.07%
        F1-Score: 0.5617
        ROC-AUC: 0.8172
        Assessment: Acceptable baseline, but inferior recall due to rigid axis-aligned decision partitions.

    K-Nearest Neighbors (K = 7):

        Accuracy: 77.22%
        Precision: 57.73%
        Recall: 50.80%
        F1-Score: 0.5404
        ROC-AUC: 0.7902
        Assessment: Lowest discriminative capability; suffers from distance distortion in a 30-dimensional encoded feature space.
