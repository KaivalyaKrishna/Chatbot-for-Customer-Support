5. Diagnostic Visualizations & Error Analysis

    5.1 Confusion Matrix Analysis (Champion Model: Random Forest)
    
        The Confusion Matrix computed on the test set highlights the distribution of classification outcomes:

            True Negatives (TN): 925 retained customers were correctly identified as staying.
            False Positives (FP): 110 retained customers were incorrectly predicted to churn. In business terms, this represents a low operational cost (offering a small promotional incentive to a customer who wasn't leaving).
            False Negatives (FN): 169 churning customers were missed by the model. This represents the primary business vulnerability, as these accounts leave without proactive retention outreach.
            True Positives (TP): 205 churning customers were successfully flagged, allowing customer retention teams to intervene in advance.

    5.2 Comparative ROC Curve Analysis
    
        The Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC) curve evaluates model performance across all possible probability thresholds (0.0 to 1.0):

        Both Random Forest and Logistic Regression demonstrated excellent discrimination curves, substantially outperforming the diagonal random baseline (AUC = 0.50).
        The Random Forest achieved the highest Area Under the Curve (AUC = 0.8445), maintaining a superior True Positive Rate at lower False Positive rates compared to KNN and individual Decision Trees.
