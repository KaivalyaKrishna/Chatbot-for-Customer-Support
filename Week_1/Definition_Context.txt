2.1 The Business Problem
    When customers discontinue their service (churn), the business loses steady recurring revenue and risks market share loss. Currently, retention strategies are purely reactive—customer service reaches out only after a cancellation request has been filed. The goal is to shift to a predictive retention strategy that identifies at-risk accounts 30 to 60 days before they decide to leave.

2.2 Machine Learning Problem Formulation
    Mathematically, this problem is framed as:

        1. Supervised Binary Classification: Predicting the probability 𝑃 (Churn = 1 ∣ 𝑋), where target Y ∈ {0,1} (0 = Retained, 1 = Churned).
        2. Unsupervised Behavioral Segmentation: Discovering K natural clusters among customers based on tenure, service usage, and billing patterns without pre-labeled categories.

2.3 Pipeline Inputs (Feature Schema)
    The machine learning model will ingest customer attributes across three primary categories:

        1. Customer Demographics: Senior citizen status, partner/dependent status.
        2. Account & Subscription Information: Account tenure (in months), contract type (month-to-month, one-year, two-year), payment method, and paperless billing.
        3. Services Subscribed: Internet service type (DSL, Fiber optic), online security, tech support, streaming TV/movies.
        4. Financial Metrics: Monthly charges ($ amount) and total lifetime charges ($ amount).

2.4 Technical Challenges

    1. Class Imbalance: Typically, only 20–25% of customers churn in a given period. If an algorithm simply predicts "No Churn" for every customer, it achieves 75–80% accuracy while failing completely at its business goal.
    2. Data Quality & Missing Values: Financial fields like total charges often have whitespace or missing entries for brand-new customers with 0 months of tenure.
    3. Mixed Data Types: High proportion of categorical variables requiring appropriate encoding techniques (One-Hot Encoding vs. Ordinal Encoding).

2.5 Expected Outcomes & Business Deliverables

    1. A trained, calibrated classification model delivering an F1-score ≥ 0.80 and ROC-AUC ≥ 0.85.
    2. Defined customer personas (e.g., Budget Month-to-Month High Risk, Long-Term Stable Bundled Users).
    3. A lightweight REST API endpoint that accepts customer data in JSON format and outputs:
        • Churn Probability (e.g., 87%)
        • Risk Category (High, Medium, Low)
        • Recommended Customer Segment for targeted promotions.
