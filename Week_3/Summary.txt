1. Executive Summary & Week 3 Objectives

    The Week 3 milestone expands the customer churn system into unsupervised customer discovery and advanced model optimization. While supervised models predict individual churn likelihood, unsupervised clustering reveals the underlying behavioral personas driving customer actions.
    
    Key objectives achieved in Week 3:
        Applied Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to compress high-dimensional feature spaces for 2D visualization.
        Implemented K-Means clustering, determining the optimal number of customer personas (KKK) using the Elbow Method and Silhouette Analysis.
        Constructed Hierarchical Agglomerative Clustering dendrograms using Ward’s linkage.
        Profiled extracted customer clusters into actionable business personas based on tenure, monthly spending, and churn rates.
        Executed systematic hyperparameter tuning on the champion Random Forest classifier using 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation (GridSearchCV).
