2. Dimensionality Reduction with Principal Component Analysis (PCA)
    
    With 30 preprocessed feature dimensions, direct geometric visualization of customer behavior is impossible. 
    Principal Component Analysis was implemented to identify the orthogonal axes of maximum variance:

        PCA(n_components=2) rotated the multidimensional space into two uncorrelated components (PC1 and PC2).
        The top two components successfully captured the dominant variance, enabling effective 2D scatter plots of cluster boundaries without distorting natural customer relationships.
