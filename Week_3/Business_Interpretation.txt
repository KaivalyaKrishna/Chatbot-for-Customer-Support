4. Customer Persona Profiles & Business Interpretation

    Aggregating the original customer attributes by their assigned K-Means cluster revealed three highly distinct customer personas:
    
        Cluster 0 - High-Risk New Subscribers: Characterized by low average tenure (under 12 months), month-to-month contracts, and an alarming churn rate exceeding 45%. This group requires immediate onboarding support and early loyalty incentives.    
            Action: Implement automated 30-day and 60-day customer onboarding check-ins, offer a discount for transitioning from month-to-month to an annual contract, and provide complimentary tech support for the first 3 months. 


        Cluster 1 - High-Value Long-Term Champions: Characterized by multi-year tenure (40+ months), bundled services (fiber optic, streaming), high monthly spend, and a minimal churn rate under 10%. This group represents the core recurring revenue of the business.
            Action: Enroll in VIP loyalty programs, offer early access to new service upgrades (e.g., 5G/Fiber speed boosts), and maintain proactive customer service to safeguard high monthly recurring revenue. 

 
        Cluster 2 - Low-Touch Budget Subscribers: Characterized by moderate tenure, basic DSL or phone-only service, low monthly charges, and moderate churn stability.
            Action: Avoid aggressive upselling that might trigger cancellations; introduce flexible low-cost digital payment methods and automated billing alerts.
